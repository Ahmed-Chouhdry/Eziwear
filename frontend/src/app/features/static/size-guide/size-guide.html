Between sizes?

If your measurement falls between two sizes, we recommend sizing up for a comfortable, relaxed fit — true to the EZiWear silhouette. Colours may vary slightly by screen, and finished pieces can differ by ±0.5–1" from the chart due to hand-finishing.
